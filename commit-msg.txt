perf(rdp): move a scrolled region instead of repainting it

Diffing halved the traffic, 7.7 MB to 3.9 MB, but it cannot help a
scroll: every pixel in the viewport really does change, so the diff
correctly reports all of it and we encode all of it. That is the whole
of the remaining gap to guacd, whose advantage here is not its codec but
that it never sends those pixels at all.

A damage rectangle is now tested for a vertical shift against the
previous frame. When one is found the client is told to move what it
already holds, and only the strip the scroll revealed is encoded.

Accepting a shift that is not really there would tell the browser to
move the wrong pixels, and the result would stay on screen until
something else repainted it. The guard against that is deliberately
strict: the probe row must not be a single flat colour, the shift must
be an exact row match, and at least four sampled rows across the
rectangle must match exactly before it is trusted. A rejected candidate
costs one comparison and falls back to diffing.

Also reports encoder totals on drop. The periodic report is useless for
a short session: a minute of measuring produced no summary at all, and
the figures had to be reconstructed from raw log lines.
